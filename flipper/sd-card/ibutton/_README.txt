==============================
 iBUTTON  (Dallas contact keys)
==============================
The round metal "coin" keys you touch to a
reader - intercoms, elevators, building doors,
gym lockers.

COPY YOUR OWN KEY
1. iButton > Read
2. Touch the key to the Flipper's iButton pads
   (top-left GPIO pins, pin 17/18 area)
3. Save -> Emulate to act as the key

RULE: your own keys or permission.
