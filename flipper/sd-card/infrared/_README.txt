==============================
 INFRARED  (IR blaster)
==============================
Controls anything with an IR remote:
TVs, ACs, soundbars, projectors, LED strips.

BUILT-IN (Infrared menu)
- Universal Remotes > TV  = TV-B-Gone.
    Open, hold OK, it fires every TV-off code.
    Point at any TV to kill it.
- Universal Remotes > AC / Audio / Projector
- Learn New Remote: point your real remote at
    the Flipper's TOP edge, press a button, save.
- Saved / IRDB = big library of remotes.

APPS (Apps > Infrared)
- XRemote ......... build one custom remote from
    buttons of many devices
- IR Intervalometer  wireless camera shutter /
    timelapse (Canon / Nikon / Sony)

PRANKS (own / friends)
- Kill a TV in a room (TV-B-Gone)
- Crank volume / flip channels from afar
- Strobe / recolor an RGB LED strip

RULE: your stuff or friends only.
