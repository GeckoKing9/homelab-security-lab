==============================
 FLIPPER CHEAT SHEET (read first)
==============================
Device: Whadin3n

Every folder has a _README.txt = what it is,
how to use, examples. Open it: Archive (the
folder icon) > pick a folder > _README.txt.

------------------------------
 EVERYDAY (no WiFi board needed)
------------------------------
- Universal TV / AC remote:
    Infrared > Universal Remotes
- Copy your own fob / key:
    RFID 125kHz > Read > Save > Emulate
- Offline 2FA codes:
    Apps > Tools > Authenticator
- Loyalty-card barcodes on screen:
    Apps > Tools > Barcode Generator
- Find a lost bag (AirTag-style):
    Apps > Bluetooth > FindMy Flipper
