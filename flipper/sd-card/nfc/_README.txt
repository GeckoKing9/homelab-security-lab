==============================
 NFC  (13.56 MHz tap cards)
==============================
Tap cards, transit cards, hotel keys,
office badges, bank cards, Amiibo.

BUILT-IN (NFC menu)
- Read ..... scan a card, save it
- Saved .... emulate a saved card
- NFC Maker  write URL / WiFi / contact to a tag

APPS (Apps > NFC)
- NFC Magic ...... clone onto "magic" cards
- MFKey32 ........ recover MIFARE keys from a
    reader -> then clone a secured card
- Seader ......... iCLASS SE / SAM cards
- Picopass ....... HID iCLASS read/write
- Access Auditor . tap a badge -> weak or
    secure? gives a risk score

BANK CARD DEMO (harmless eye-opener)
NFC > Read a contactless card: shows the card
number + expiry it broadcasts. It does NOT show
the CVV and CANNOT charge it. Just proof of what
leaks over the air.

CLONE YOUR OWN CARD
Read it. EM4100 / MIFARE Classic copy easily.
DESFire / iCLASS SE = secure, won't clone.

TAP vs FOB?
- Thin card / phone tap = NFC (here)
- Thick fob / old card  = RFID 125kHz (lfrfid)

RULE: your own cards or permission.
